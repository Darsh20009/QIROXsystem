Approved with modifications.

Design System V2 (White / Off White / Navy / Blue / Black / Gray + limited Dark Green) is now the permanent design language for the entire platform.

However, do NOT think of this as a website redesign.

This is a complete Brand Experience redesign.

The objective is that every visitor immediately feels that QIROX is a real premium technology company with real people, real products, real partners, and real engineering culture.

Before implementing any page, follow these additional rules.

====================================================
RULE 1 — ONE DESIGN LANGUAGE
====================================================

Every page in the platform must look like it belongs to the same company.

No page may have a different visual identity.

Everything must share:

- spacing
- typography
- iconography
- motion
- cards
- buttons
- borders
- shadows
- glass
- lighting
- 3D language

====================================================
RULE 2 — EVERY PAGE TELLS A STORY
====================================================

Every page must have:

Beginning

Middle

Ending

Clear CTA

Purpose

Emotion

Nothing should exist just because templates usually have it.

RULE 3 — HUMAN FIRST
====================================================

No AI feeling.

No repeated layouts.

No generic gradients.

No fake people.

No fake offices.

No fake testimonials.

No fake statistics.
