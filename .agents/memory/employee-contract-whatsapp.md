Employment agreements are drafts, not legal advice. Require verified employer details and review by qualified Saudi counsel or authorized HR before signing. Include the company rights selected for the project: ownership of work product created within the employee's role, confidentiality of company/client information, and return of company assets plus access revocation at termination.

**Why:** The user selected Saudi jurisdiction and these rights, while an app-generated template cannot verify the employer's legal entity or establish legal compliance.

**How to apply:** Keep role-change and termination notices review-before-send. Termination must revoke access immediately and preserve the employment record. Subscription reminders are sent manually; employee recipients must be assigned to the selected project. Use the existing QIROX WhatsApp connection.
