---
name: Email inbox alert routing
description: Routing policy for notifications caused by incoming email to QIROX mailboxes.
---

Incoming-mail notifications must not be sent to the CTO through WhatsApp or in-app alerts. Keep the emails accessible in their mailboxes and preserve notifications to other configured recipients. New mail to the y.business mailbox has no executive notification recipient unless the user changes this policy.

**Why:** The user asked that messages arriving at any QIROX email account stop generating notifications for the CTO.

**How to apply:** Keep the rule scoped to inbound-mail monitoring. Do not remove CTO notifications for unrelated events such as orders or account security notices.
